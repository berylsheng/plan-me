## Description (≤ 4000) — 1412 chars
```text
Plan-Me is a family planner you write in plain words. Type a sentence and it becomes an event — no forms, no fiddly date pickers.

WRITE IT THE WAY YOU SAY IT
• "tomorrow 9am dentist"
• "6 June, 6-8pm, dancing"
• "next Friday 12pm lunch"
• "swim Tue 7am @AL, weekly for 2 months"
Dates, times, time ranges, people and repeats are all picked up from the sentence. Tap Tips any time to see everything it understands.

REPEATING EVENTS
Daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or every few days, weeks or months, for up to a year. Change one occurrence or all the ones after it.

SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY
Create a family and share the invite code. Each event is yours alone or shared with the family — you choose when you add it. Family members can see and edit shared events, and changes appear on everyone's phone as they happen.

KNOW WHO IT'S FOR
Tag family members with @ and their photo or tag shows on the event. Shared events show who added them.

A MORNING SUMMARY
Turn it on in Settings and Plan-Me tells you each morning what's on that day, at a time you choose.

WORKS OFFLINE
Your planner opens and works without a connection. Changes sync when you're back online.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Personal events are only ever visible to you. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Plan-Me needs a free account so your events can be shared and kept safe.
```
