Plan-Me

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Plan-Me is a family planner for iPhone, made by Beryl Sheng, an individual developer in New Zealand. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: berylsheng@gmail.com.

In short: Plan-Me keeps only what it needs to run your planner and share it with your family. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

What Plan-Me stores

Your account

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that can't be read back.
  • Your display name, if you add one.

Your profile

  • Your tag (two to four letters, such as @AL) and your profile photo, if you choose one.

Your family

  • The family's name, its invite code, and who is in it.

Your events

  • Each event's name, date, start and end time, how it repeats, the people tagged on it, and whether it is personal or shared.

On your phone

  • A copy of your events, so the planner works offline.
  • Your sign-in session, kept encrypted in the iPhone's secure storage.
  • Your morning summary setting. The morning summary notification is created on your phone; its content isn't sent anywhere.

What Plan-Me doesn't collect

  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • Photos: the app only reads the one photo you pick for your profile.

Who can see your information

  • Personal events are visible only to you.
  • Shared events are visible to everyone in your family, who can also edit them.
  • Your display name, tag and profile photo are visible to the members of your family.
  • Nobody outside your family can see your events or profile.

Services Plan-Me uses

These companies process information only to run the app:

  • Supabase hosts the account system, database and profile photos, in its Sydney, Australia data centre.
  • An email delivery service sends the sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails to your email address.
  • Expo delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo's servers receive standard technical details such as your IP address and device type.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database only lets each person read their own data and their family's shared data.

How long it's kept

Your information is kept while you have an account. Signing out removes your events and settings from that phone; they stay in your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes:

  • your account (email and password), profile and profile photo
  • your personal events
  • your place in your family

Events you shared with your family stay with the family, shown as created by a "Former member". If you were the last person in the family, those are deleted too. You can also ask for deletion by emailing berylsheng@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

Your rights

You can see and change your name, tag, photo and events in the app at any time. Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for a copy of your information or for it to be corrected; email berylsheng@gmail.com. If you're not happy with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

Plan-Me is made for families. Children should use it with a parent or guardian's permission. It collects nothing beyond what this policy describes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
