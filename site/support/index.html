Common questions

How do I add an event?

Type a sentence in the box at the bottom of the planner and tap Add, for example 6pm swim @AL, weekly for 4 weeks. Tap (?) Tips above the box for everything it understands.

How do I change an event?

Tap Modify, then Edit on the event. For a repeating event you can change just that one or all the ones after it.

How do I share events with my family?

One person creates a family on the Family tab and shares the invite code; everyone else joins with that code. Then choose Family instead of Only me when you add or edit an event.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder. On the sign-in screen, try to sign in and tap Resend confirmation email. Open the newest email on your iPhone and tap its link once.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password, then open the email on your iPhone to choose a new one.

The morning summary doesn't arrive

Check Settings → Morning summary is on, and that notifications for Plan-Me are allowed in the iPhone's Settings app. Open Plan-Me now and then so the next two weeks stay scheduled.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for exactly what is deleted. You can also email berylsheng@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
